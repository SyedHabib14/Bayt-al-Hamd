## Welcome to Bayt al-Ḥamd

**Bayt al-Ḥamd** is a calm digital home for reading, preserving, and returning to knowledge that nourishes the heart.

It brings together **majālis**, **books**, **ziyārāt**, and **munājāt** in an experience shaped by dignity, clarity, and quiet beauty. Nothing is designed to compete for attention. Everything is designed to help the reader arrive, slow down, and remain present.

> A digital archive should feel less like a database and more like opening a carefully kept manuscript.

The visual language draws from **manuscripts, parchment, deep green, restrained gold, soft glass, and generous space**. The result is scholarly without feeling distant, luxurious without feeling excessive, and modern without losing warmth.

## For readers

Bayt al-Ḥamd is for anyone who wants to:

- read without visual noise;
- explore sacred and scholarly material in one place;
- move naturally between Arabic and English titles;
- revisit a majlis, book, ziyārat, or munājāt;
- enjoy a reading space that respects both the text and the reader.

<details>
<summary><strong>What does a visit feel like?</strong></summary>

## Behind the archive

A private editorial space allows authorised custodians to keep the collection clear and current. Majālis can be prepared, books can be catalogued, and devotional texts can be reviewed before they become part of the public archive.

For the reader, that work remains quietly in the background. What appears in front is a collection shaped with intention.

## A note on purpose

Bayt al-Ḥamd is not built around endless scrolling, urgency, or noise.

It is built around **return**: returning to a passage, a gathering, a prayer, a book, or a thought worth carrying forward.

> May every visit leave the reader with something worth remembering.
